# Riverside Planetarium — Knowledge Base

## General Information
Riverside Planetarium is a public science center dedicated to astronomy, space exploration, and earth sciences. We are located at 400 Stargazer Boulevard, Riverside, CA 92501. You can reach us by phone at (951) 555-0192 or by email at info@riversideplanetarium.org.

## Opening Hours
We are open Tuesday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays and on the following public holidays: Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day.

The last show admission is 45 minutes before closing time.

## Shows & Programs

### Cosmic Voyage (All Ages)
A 45-minute journey through the solar system, from the surface of the Sun to the icy edges of the Kuiper Belt. Narrated with stunning visuals projected on our 360-degree dome.
- Showtime: Tuesday–Friday at 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM | Saturday–Sunday at 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Suitable for: All ages (great for children 5 and up)

### Dark Universe (General Audience)
An exploration of dark matter, dark energy, and the large-scale structure of the universe. Narrated by a renowned astrophysicist.
- Showtime: Tuesday–Friday at 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM | Saturday–Sunday at 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 7:00 PM
- Duration: 50 minutes
- Suitable for: Ages 10 and up

### Stars of the Pharaohs (General Audience)
Discover how ancient Egyptians used the night sky to guide agriculture, religion, and architecture. A blend of history and astronomy.
- Showtime: Saturday–Sunday at 3:00 PM
- Duration: 40 minutes
- Suitable for: Ages 8 and up

### Tiny Stargazers (Children)
A gentle, interactive introduction to the night sky designed for young children. Features sing-along segments and a live Q&A with a planetarium educator.
- Showtime: Saturday–Sunday at 10:00 AM
- Duration: 30 minutes
- Suitable for: Ages 2–6

### Laser Nights: Classic Rock (Adults)
An immersive laser light show set to classic rock music under the dome.
- Showtime: Friday and Saturday at 8:00 PM
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Suitable for: Ages 14 and up (adult content — not suitable for young children)

## Booking & Tickets
Tickets can be purchased online at www.riversideplanetarium.org/tickets or at the box office on the day of your visit. Online booking is recommended, especially on weekends. Tickets are non-refundable but can be exchanged for a future date if cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.

## Facilities
- Free parking is available in the main lot on Stargazer Boulevard (200 spaces).
- The Nebula Café on site serves light meals, snacks, and beverages. Open during regular business hours.
- The Stardust Gift Shop offers astronomy books, telescopes, educational toys, and souvenirs. Open during regular business hours.
- Fully accessible facilities including wheelchair-accessible entrances, restrooms, and seating areas in the dome theater.
- Assistive listening devices are available at the box office at no extra charge.

## Special Events
- Star Party Nights: First Friday of every month, 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Bring your own telescope or use ours. Free with planetarium admission.
- School Programs: Curriculum-aligned programs for K–12 groups available Tuesday–Friday mornings. Advance booking required. Contact education@riversideplanetarium.org.
- Private Events: The dome theater is available for private hire on Monday evenings. Contact events@riversideplanetarium.org for pricing and availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I bring food into the dome theater?
A: No outside food or drinks are permitted inside the dome theater. The Nebula Café is available before and after shows.

Q: Is photography allowed during shows?
A: Photography and video recording are not permitted during shows out of respect for other guests and to protect the projection equipment. Photos are welcome in the lobby and exhibit areas.

Q: How early should I arrive?
A: We recommend arriving at least 15 minutes before your show. Doors to the dome close 5 minutes after show start and latecomers cannot be admitted.

Q: Are the shows in 3D?
A: No, our shows are full-dome digital projection — an immersive 360-degree experience on a domed ceiling. No 3D glasses are required.

Q: Is the planetarium suitable for people with sensory sensitivities?
A: Yes. We offer a Sensory-Friendly showing of Cosmic Voyage on the first Sunday of every month at 10:30 AM. Lights are kept slightly higher, sound is reduced, and movement in the dome is minimized.

Q: Do you have a lost and found?
A: Yes, lost and found items are held at the front desk for 30 days. Contact us at info@riversideplanetarium.org or call (951) 555-0192.
